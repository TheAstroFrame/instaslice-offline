Offline lunar surface atlas

Reference features were generated from unlabelled NASA Scientific
Visualization Studio imagery and orientation data from Moon Phase and
Libration, 2024. Credits to NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio,
Ernie Wright, the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter, and the LRO instrument
teams for the underlying lunar maps.

Reference https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5187/
Media use https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
Feature extraction uses VLFeat 0.9.21. See VLFEAT-LICENSE.txt.
Additional feature names and positions come from the IAU Gazetteer of
Planetary Nomenclature maintained by the USGS Astrogeology Science
Center. Coordinates use the LOLA 2011 lunar reference frame.
Gazetteer https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/GIS_Downloads

The atlas contains lunar landmark locations and descriptors. Imported
photos and their matches are processed in the browser.
