Andromeda close-up star-pattern index
===================================

sky-index-andromeda.bin.gz is a derived subset of Gaia DR3 (G <= 13.5) and
includes bright Hipparcos 2 stars from the tetra3rs merged catalogue. The
Gaia rows were selected around RA 10.6847°, Dec +41.2690°, within 7°, through
the Heidelberg Gaia archive mirror. The generation script propagates star
positions to epoch J2000 and computes geometric patterns for the browser solver.
