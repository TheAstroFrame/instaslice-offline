Deep-sky regional star-pattern index
====================================

sky-index-deepsky.bin.gz uses public Gaia DR3 astrometry and bright
Hipparcos 2 stars from the tetra3rs merged catalogue. The Gaia data were
retrieved through the Heidelberg Gaia archive mirror in fixed regions:

The generator propagates catalogue coordinates to epoch J2000 and stores
geometric star patterns for matching in the browser.
