InstaSlice Studio — annotation catalogues

OpenNGC (Mattia Verga and contributors)
New General Catalogue, Index Catalogue and Messier cross-identifications.
The app uses the existing CC BY-SA 4.0 adapted data in plate/openngc.json.
See plate/ANNOTATION-NOTICE.txt for credits, source revisions and changes.
https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC

Stellarium standard deep-sky catalogue 3.23 (Stellarium contributors)
An independent GPL-2.0-or-later catalogue asset from the Stellarium main
repository. Includes cross-identifications such as Caldwell, Barnard,
Sharpless, Lynds, van den Bergh, RCW, vdBH, Cederblad, PN G, SNR G,
Trumpler, Ruprecht, Collinder, PGC, UGC, ESO, Arp and Hickson.
The adaptation keeps celestial positions, selected sizes and magnitudes,
types, cross-identifications and well-known names. It omits unpositioned
or unidentifiable records, converts the data to UTF-8 JSON and uses a
separate scientific reference for the IC 1396A feature.
https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/e4a447eb504b3a731a2c7cf32292b0263d062d63/nebulae/default
Source data:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Stellarium/stellarium/e4a447eb504b3a731a2c7cf32292b0263d062d63/nebulae/default/catalog.txt

HYG Database v4.1 (David Nash / Astronomy Nexus)
CC BY-SA 4.0. The local subset includes stars to V magnitude 8.5 and stars
with proper names, with HIP, HD, HR, Bayer and Flamsteed designations when
available. Right ascension is expressed in degrees. See the existing
annotation notice for upstream Hipparcos, Yale Bright Star and Gliese credits.
https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/tree/main/hyg
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
A standalone regeneration script for both derived assets is build_catalogues.py.

Representative annotation anchors
IC 1396A, the Elephant’s Trunk dark globule, is distinct from its parent
complex IC 1396. Its marker uses the centre of Spitzer’s optical image
as a representative location on the visible globule. Its catalogue
cross-identification LBN 452 comes from SIMBAD. Reflection nebula vdB 142
and emission region Sh 2-131 have separate marker positions.
https://simbad.u-strasbg.fr/simbad/sim-id?Ident=IC+1396A
https://www.spitzer.caltech.edu/image/ssc2003-06a2-dark-globule-in-ic-1396
https://simbad.u-strasbg.fr/simbad/sim-id?Ident=Sh+2-131

The Cone Nebula is the pillar at the southern end of NGC 2264 and is not listed
separately in the catalogues above. Its marker was added by hand at 06h 41m 15s,
+09° 21′ (the position given on Wikipedia) with an approximate size of 8′, and
placed on the pillar in a plate-solved photograph.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cone_Nebula
