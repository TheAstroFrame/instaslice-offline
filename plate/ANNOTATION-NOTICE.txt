Annotation catalogues

openngc.json is adapted from OpenNGC by Mattia Verga and contributors.
Source: https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC
Inputs: database_files/NGC.csv at be3cd6c5498806a2fbb2f7e0452ef802dbb02be0
and database_files/addendum.csv at 9fce2e8a9696b53f5954d7fc216df9f6c56398af.
Upstream contributors and data sources: https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC
Changes: removed duplicate, nonexistent and unpositioned records, selected a
subset of columns, used one common name per object, added two common-name
aliases, converted coordinates to decimal degrees, and encoded the result
as compact JSON.

named-stars.json is adapted from HYG Database v4.1 by David Nash / Astronomy Nexus.
Source: https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/tree/main/hyg
Input: hyg/CURRENT/hygdata_v41.csv at 3bf37f4b2d5460e1278286320d1d62fab9b493c1.
HYG incorporates Hipparcos, Yale Bright Star and Gliese catalogues. Proper
names are based on the IAU Catalog of Star Names with HYG additions.
Changes: selected named stars, omitted the Sun, selected a subset of columns,
converted right ascension to decimal degrees and encoded the result as JSON.

Both JSON adaptations are distributed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Object rows contain ID, common name, type code, J2000 right ascension and
declination (degrees), visual magnitude (B magnitude when V is unavailable in
OpenNGC), and major-axis diameter (arcminutes). Missing values are null.
