InstaSlice Beta star solver and public sky data
================================================

InstaSlice implements this solver and its index builder in this directory.
It uses four-star sky patterns to estimate plate scale, orientation and sky
position. It checks the result against other stars before passing a WCS
solution to InstaSlice Studio's annotation tools.

The deep-sky index covers selected fields in both hemispheres, including
Andromeda, Triangulum, 30 Doradus, the Pleiades, the Trifid and Eagle Nebulae,
Orion, the Veil, the Elephant's Trunk, and several galaxy and nebula fields
in the OpenNGC catalogue. Very narrow images beyond these regions can have
insufficient catalogue stars. The wide-field index covers the whole sky
with the bright subset of Gaia DR3 and the Hipparcos 2 supplement from the
public measurement table distributed by tetra3rs. See ../DATA-CREDITS.txt
for data release and citation details.

Credit: ESA/Gaia/DPAC. This work includes data from the Gaia mission,

National institutions, in particular the institutions participating in

The index files contain catalogue astrometry and generated star patterns.
Their data terms are Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0 IGO.
The original test photographs were used locally for calibration and
verification. Their pixels, metadata and detected stars are absent from
the distributed files.
