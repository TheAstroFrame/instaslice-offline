InstaSlice Studio offline star-pattern index
===========================================

The sky-index.bin.gz and sky-index-narrow.bin.gz files are computed,
magnitude-limited star-pattern indexes adapted from the tetra3rs Gaia DR3 and Hipparcos 2 merged star catalogue.
The build code selects bright stars, propagates coordinates to epoch J2000,
computes geometric patterns and saves the resulting database in tetra3 format.
