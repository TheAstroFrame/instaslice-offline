Close-field star-pattern index
==============================

sky-index-small-fields.bin.gz uses public Gaia DR3 astrometry and bright
Hipparcos 2 stars from the tetra3rs merged catalogue. The index supplements
the all-sky indexes around these deep-sky fields.

The build propagates catalogue positions to J2000 and indexes the angular
geometry of groups of four stars. The browser compares those patterns
with stars detected locally in the photograph.
